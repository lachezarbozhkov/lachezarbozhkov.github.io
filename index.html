Experience

Dext · Head of AI, hands-on technical lead

2023 – present

In 2023 I went back to hands-on engineering to lead Dext's move to LLM-based extraction. I work with a small senior team and designed and built most of the production LLM platform myself.

  • Designed and built the LLM extraction platform behind eight production flows: one gateway protocol over Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI Gemini and AWS Bedrock Claude, a declarative model registry with date-aware pricing, deterministic weighted routing, cross-provider failover and request-scoped deadlines. Request errors fell from 6.6% to 1.3%, and adding a model or provider is a configuration change.
  • Took bank-statement extraction from research to production in four weeks, then raised reconciliation from 84% to 94% (98.8% on the automatic channel) with a reconciliation-gated model cascade and arithmetic post-processing, while cutting cost per statement by 42% and median latency by 56%. 79% of reviewed statements no longer need a human.
  • Internationalised line-item extraction from a French-only flow to eight markets: document-perfect rate 62–77% → 85–89% at 23–35% lower cost, confirmed with paired statistical tests and a live A/B before rollout.
  • Built the evaluation discipline: DVC-tracked benchmarks of thousands of labelled statements and invoices, offline replay of any cascade policy from cached runs in seconds, an agent-based labelling protocol with blind audits, and the rule that the labelling model's family is never an evaluation arm. Benchmarked 12+ models across three providers.
  • Production ownership end to end: structured output via forced tool calls with typed validation and automatic repair, Prometheus and Grafana dashboards for cost, failover and cascade stages, gradual rollouts with explicit pause criteria, EU data-residency routing.
  • Automation gains across all extraction flows worth millions of pounds a year in avoided manual review, and the lever that let operations scale without matching headcount. Wrote the briefs operations used to plan vendor ramp-down.
